# roblox-project-showcase

This is my Roblox project inspired by DayZ.
Genre: Open-world multiplayer survival game with shooter elements

https://youtu.be/cZRmX3PH_aY?si=F4Y4zr8CTwpHxETE

What Was Implemented

* **Physical Ballistics & Shooting System:** Realistic weapon mechanics accounting for bullet drop, travel time, and ballistics.
* **Survival Mechanics:** Dynamic hunger and thirst systems requiring players to scavenge for food and water.
* **Advanced Zombie AI:** Realistic infected behavior, including detection, pathfinding, and pursuit mechanics.
* **Inventory & Loot System:** Grid/slot-based inventory management for organizing gear, weapons, and survival resources.
* **Squad / Party System:** Functionality allowing players to create groups and team up with friends.
* **Navigation:** Functional in-game compass for orientation in the open world.
* **Realistic Health & Medical System:** Detailed injury mechanics and a comprehensive healing system.
* **Custom Character Controller:** Modified movement mechanics and improved player dynamics.
* **Open-World Map:** Custom-built game environment designed for exploration and survival.

🚧 *Other features and systems are currently in active development.*
